Before disease, pain, and suffering entered the world, God designed a lifestyle that would bring humanity joy, strength, and perfect health. He placed our first parents in a garden, rich in life and nourishment. Returning to God’s original plan is more than a health trend—it is a spiritual journey back to His perfect will.

## 1. God’s Original Diet — Perfect and Life-giving

In Eden, God gave Adam and Eve a simple, plant‑based diet filled with living foods from nature.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Genesis 1:29"
>
  And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed... and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.
</Quote>

After sin, vegetables were added to supply additional nourishment:

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Genesis 3:18"
>
  Thou shalt eat the herb of the field.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"

  Grains, fruits, nuts, and vegetables constitute the diet chosen for us by our Creator.
</Quote>

## 4. Eden Health Is More Than Diet

The Eden lifestyle consisted of pure air, sunlight, temperance, rest, exercise, water, good food, and trust in God. These same principles are still the foundation of vibrant health today.

- Pure fresh air  
- Sunlight  
- Rest & Sabbath refreshment  
- Exercise  
- Pure water  
- Natural plant‑based diet  
- Temperance / self‑control  
- Trust in God  

## Conclusion

The invitation to return **Back to Eden** is an invitation to walk closer with God, to restore body, mind, and spirit. By embracing Heaven’s simple plan, we prepare not only for better health today, but for the soon coming of Christ.

**Returning to Eden is not just a diet—it’s discipleship.**
