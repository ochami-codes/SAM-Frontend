Temperance is more than moderation in eating and drinking; it is the exercise of self‑control in every aspect of life. God designed humans with the capacity to master impulses, restrain desires, and live in harmony with His laws. Practicing temperance is both a health principle and a spiritual discipline.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"
  reference="p. 234"
>
  Temperance is the law of self-control, the practice of moderation, the guiding principle of all right habits.
</Quote>

## 1. The Bible on Self-Control

Scripture emphasizes that self‑control is a mark of spiritual maturity and a vital part of honoring God with our lives.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="2 Timothy 1:7"
>
  For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Galatians 5:22–23"
>
  But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="sop"

>
  The youth should be taught that in the exercise of temperance, they are training themselves to control the powers of mind and body.
</Quote>

## 2. Temperance in Eating and Drinking

God created the body to function best when we follow His dietary principles. Overindulgence, excessive stimulants, or harmful substances weaken both body and mind.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="Counsels on Diet and Foods"
  reference="p. 40"
>
  The excessive use of food or drink, or indulgence in unhealthful habits, diminishes the strength and energy of the body, and lessens the moral power.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Proverbs 23:2"
>
  And put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.
</Quote>

## 3. Self-Control in Thoughts and Actions

True temperance goes beyond food—it encompasses the mind and emotions. Restraining anger, lust, envy, and selfish desires is as important as controlling the appetite. Self‑control protects the temple of God and preserves moral and spiritual health.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"
  reference="p. 234"
>
  Self-control is the secret of strength; he who has mastered his impulses has won a victory that no power can take from him.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Proverbs 16:32"
>
  He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.
</Quote>

## 4. Benefits of Temperance

Practicing temperance blesses both body and soul. It leads to improved health, mental clarity, emotional stability, and spiritual strength.

- Better digestion, energy, and immunity  
- Sharper mind and sound judgment  
- Resilience against temptation and bad habits  
- Closer relationship with God  

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="Counsels on Diet and Foods"
  reference="p. 41"
>
  The practice of temperance is the foundation of physical, mental, and spiritual strength.
</Quote>

Temperance and self‑control are not restrictions—they are keys to freedom. By mastering our desires and living in harmony with God’s laws, we honor Him, preserve our health, and prepare our minds and hearts for holy service.

**Self‑control is the pathway to strength, health, and spiritual victory.**
