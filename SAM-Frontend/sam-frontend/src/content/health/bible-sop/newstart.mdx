God designed humans with an intricate body and mind, meant to function in harmony with His laws. Both Scripture and Ellen White outline practical principles that safeguard health and strengthen the body. Modern science confirms these principles as essential for longevity and well‑being. These are the eight pillars of health.

## 1. Pure Air

Fresh air is vital for circulation, immunity, and brain function. God emphasized the importance of clean surroundings.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"
  reference="p. 127"
>
  Pure air is essential to health. Without it, life is feeble, and the powers of mind and body are diminished.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Genesis 2:7"
>
  And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="science"
  source="Environmental Health Perspectives"
  reference="2017"
>
  Indoor air quality affects cognitive function, cardiovascular health, and immune response.
</Quote>

## 2. Sunshine

Sunlight is essential for vitamin D production, bone health, and mood regulation. God intended humans to enjoy sunlight in moderation.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"
  reference="p. 127"
>
  Sunlight is the great antiseptic, and exposure to it invigorates both body and mind.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="science"
  source="Journal of Clinical Endocrinology"
  reference="2014"
>
  Adequate sunlight helps prevent rickets, supports the immune system, and reduces depression.
</Quote>

God’s original diet of fruits, vegetables, grains, and nuts supports optimal health and longevity.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Genesis 1:29"
>
  And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed... to you it shall be for meat.
</Quote>

<Quote

<Quote

</Quote>

Water regulates body temperature, supports digestion, and flushes toxins. God highlighted the importance of natural, clean water.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"

>
  Pure water is the great thirst quencher and life-giver.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="science"

Physical activity strengthens muscles, circulation, and mental health. God intended that humans work and move regularly.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"
  reference="p. 127"

<Quote

## 8. Trust in God

Spiritual peace and trust in God support mental and physical health. Anxiety, worry, and fear can damage the body.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="1 Peter 5:7"
>
  Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="The Ministry of Healing"
  reference="p. 128"
>
  Faith in God produces tranquility of mind and a healthy body.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="science"
  source="Psychoneuroendocrinology"
  reference="2016"

  Spiritual practices reduce stress, improve immune function, and promote longevity.
</Quote>

These eight principles—pure air, sunshine, proper nutrition, water, exercise, rest, temperance, and trust in God—form a holistic plan for health. Following them not only strengthens the body but aligns us with God’s original design, promoting longevity, joy, and spiritual readiness.
