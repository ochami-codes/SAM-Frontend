Adultery is not just about physical unfaithfulness: it begins in the heart, in words, in secret affections, and in careless actions. Today, broken boundaries, flirty words, late-night secret communications, improper gestures, and touchy hands have sadly become common even among married men and women. Yet, God calls it what it is: sin, exceedingly sinful in His sight.

## God’s Command Against Adultery

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Exodus 20:14"
>
  Thou shalt not commit adultery.
</Quote>

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Matthew 5:27–28"
>
  Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not commit adultery: But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.
</Quote>

Marriage is a sacred covenant. The affection, tender words, and gestures of love of a married person must be reserved only for their spouse. To bestow them elsewhere is to rob God and one’s partner of what rightfully belongs in marriage.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="Adventist Home"
  reference="p. 104.1"
>
  Be as true as steel to your marriage vows, refusing, in thought, word, or deed, to spoil your record as a man who fears God and obeys His commandments.
</Quote>

## The Sacredness of Marriage

Marriage vows are not mere words spoken at the altar: they are solemn promises before God. To trifle with them is to dishonor heaven.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="Adventist Home"
  reference="p. 341"
>
  The law of God is plainly written in the New Testament Scriptures. Adultery is there mentioned as one of the sins that will close the gates of the Holy City against the transgressor.
</Quote>

The marriage bed must be kept pure. God requires faithfulness not only in deeds but also in thoughts and attitudes.

## Why is Adultery Prevalent in the Church?

Tragically, worldliness has crept into the church. Fashion displays, low necklines, tight dresses, short skirts, and revealing clothing have turned the house of God into a place of temptation rather than reverence.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="Child Guidance"
  reference="p. 432"
>
  Fashion is deteriorating the morals of the youth. Its influence is to weaken the mind, to destroy the love of simplicity, and to create in both young and old a passion for dress and display.
</Quote>

When professed Christians act carelessly or dress immodestly, they not only dishonor God but also become stumbling blocks to others.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Matthew 18:6"
>
  But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea.
</Quote>

## Practical Steps to Guard Against Adultery

- **Guard the Thoughts**  
  <Quote
    category="bible"
    reference="Proverbs 4:23"
  >
    Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.
  </Quote>  
  <Quote
    category="sop"
    source="Mind, Character, and Personality Vol. 2"
    reference="p. 595"
  >
    Few realize that it is a duty to exercise control over the thoughts and imagination.
  </Quote>

- **Avoid Familiarities and Tempting Situations**  
  <Quote
    category="bible"
    reference="1 Thessalonians 5:22"
  >
    Abstain from all appearance of evil.
  </Quote>  
  <Quote
    category="sop"
    source="Child Guidance"
    reference="p. 466"
  >
    Those who would preserve physical health, a sound understanding, and a good memory must flee from the excitement of forbidden pleasures.
  </Quote>

- **Dress Modestly and Encourage Modesty in the Church**  
  <Quote
    category="bible"
    reference="1 Timothy 2:9"
  >
    In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety.
  </Quote>  
  <Quote
    category="sop"
    source="Messages to Young People"
    reference="p. 344"
  >
    A modest God-fearing woman will dress modestly.
  </Quote>

- **Cherish and Nurture Your Marriage**  
  <Quote
    category="sop"
    source="Adventist Home"
    reference="p. 25"
  >
    Marriage has received Christ’s sanction and blessing, and it is to be regarded as a sacred institution.
  </Quote>

- **Pray and Stay Connected to Christ**  
  <Quote
    category="bible"
    reference="Matthew 26:41"
  >
    Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.
  </Quote>  
  <Quote
    category="sop"
    source="Great Controversy"
    reference="p. 511"
  >
    The strongest temptation cannot excuse sin. However great the pressure brought to bear upon the soul, transgression is our own act.
  </Quote>

Adultery: in thoughts, words, or actions, is an abomination before God. It destroys homes, cripples the church’s witness, and brings reproach upon the name of Christ. Let us guard the sacredness of marriage, uphold purity in the church, and honor God by living faithfully. May husbands and wives be “true as steel” to their vows, and may the church reflect Christ’s holiness in every word, deed, and appearance.
