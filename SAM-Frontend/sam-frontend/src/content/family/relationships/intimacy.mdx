Sex before marriage has become so common today that even raising the question seems unnecessary to many. The world has normalized fornication to the point where what God calls sin is no longer seen as sinful, even among those who claim to follow Him. But for God’s children, the issue of premarital sex is not about culture, feelings, or convenience: it’s about faithfulness to the One we profess to serve.

## The Problem of Premarital Sex

Engaging in premarital intimacy dishonors God in several ways. It breaks His clear commandment to keep the marriage bed undefiled, undermines the purity of the covenant relationship, and opens the door for the enemy to take control of what should be a holy preparation for marriage.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="Hebrews 13:4"
>
  Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.
</Quote>

## Courtship and Spiritual Danger

Many courtships today are carried on in a way that excludes God, while giving the devil influence over relationships that should belong to Christ. A courtship not founded on prayer, purity, and respect will not produce a marriage blessed by God.

<Quote
  category="sop"
  source="Adventist Home"
  reference="p. 55.3"
>
  Courtship as carried on in this age is a scheme of deception and hypocrisy, with which the enemy of souls has far more to do than the Lord.
</Quote>

If the devil is given control of our courtship, who then will dominate the marriage? The answer is clear: unless God is invited into our courtship, He will not be the foundation of our marriage. And a marriage built on lust, secrecy, or sin cannot enjoy God’s blessing.

## True Love Waits

Courtship is not the place for physical indulgence but for spiritual preparation, mutual respect, and seeking God’s will together. A couple who honors God before marriage will invite His presence and blessing after marriage. But those who compromise in courtship will reap sorrow later.

<Quote
  category="bible"
  reference="1 Corinthians 6:19–20"
>
  Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body.
</Quote>


Purity is not just about avoiding sin; it is about glorifying God with our bodies and preserving the sacred gift of intimacy for its rightful place: within the holy covenant of marriage. When we honor God in our courtship, we secure His blessing for our future home.
